01The problem

The client wanted to capture media mentions and external recognition for inclusion in the 2025 Annual Report, but coverage was spread across websites, partner organizations, social platforms, and existing internal files.

02The plan

Search for relevant mentions, verify that each result was actually connected to the organization or its leadership, record the source and date, and organize the strongest items for the report.

04Results

Created a clearer record of external coverage and recognition that could be incorporated into the annual report and used as a reference for future communications.
